Robot Learning

Human Takeover Guided Selective DAgger for MetaWorld Push-v3

This project improves a vision-based manipulation policy by treating human takeover annotations as signals of risky states and using those signals to target expert relabeling during DAgger.

Open Source README

Main Result

  • Base policy mean success: 0.393
  • Selective DAgger mean success: 0.647
  • Takeover labels were most valuable as a state-selection signal

Project Pipeline

From base policy to predictor-guided relabeling

  1. Train a wrist-camera base policy on `push-v3`.
  2. Collect rollout episodes from that policy.
  3. Annotate risky or failed segments with human takeover intervals.
  4. Train a takeover predictor from those labeled rollouts.
  5. Use the predictor to trigger selective expert relabeling during DAgger.
  6. Finetune the policy with original data plus targeted expert labels.

Key Idea

Human takeover is more than extra demonstration data

Instead of using takeover moments only as added demonstrations, this project interprets them as warnings that the policy has entered risky states. That turns human intervention into a supervision signal for deciding where expert labels are most valuable.
